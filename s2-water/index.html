Phone test S2: water

How much water this phone can draw while keeping its frame rate. Eight steps of water, from flat to everything on, each measured with extra load until frames drop. About 8 minutes, plus an optional 10-minute heat run. Nothing leaves the phone unless you copy the card yourself.

Turn the phone sideways (landscape), then press 1. Start.

0. Before you start

  • Landscape. Portrait Orientation Lock off (Control Centre).
  • Brightness as you would play; Low Power Mode off for now.
  • Close other apps. Note the battery % below.

1. Start

Keeps the screen awake and measures the refresh rate for 2 s (the screen stays dark then).

2. Look

Tap through the steps and watch the water. Which is the cheapest step that still looks like water?

3. Ladder (about 6 min)

Prop the phone up in landscape and do not touch it. Each step plays for a few seconds with more and more hidden extra drawing until frames drop. Stop is top right.

4. Path (2 min)

The 60-second boat path with everything on, at full and at half resolution. Watch for stutter.

5. Heat run (10 min, optional)

Best after the phone lay idle and unplugged for 10 minutes. Everything on, full resolution. Note the battery % before and after. Feel the back of the phone at the end.
