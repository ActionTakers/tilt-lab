0. Before you start

1. Start

Allow motion when asked. Then hold the phone like a steering wheel and keep it still for a second: that pose becomes straight ahead. Hold the screen anywhere (not on a button) to brake and slide.

2. Practice lap

One lap with the offshore boat, camera A, no rival. Pass between each pair of buoys; the next pair glows.

3. Six runs (about 6 min)

One lap each: two boats, three cameras, a rival that drives the same rules. After each lap, score fun and comfort 0-10. Your first feeling counts.
